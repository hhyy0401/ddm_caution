# Repository conventions

- Every commit message must contain only one or two simple words.
- Do not add Codex, Claude, or any AI service as a collaborator, author, or co-author. Do not add AI co-author trailers to commits.
- Preserve the manuscript disclosure of AI assistance. It is separate from authorship.
- Never commit participant-level data, fitted participant tables, credentials, local paths, or build logs.
- Avoid em dashes in manuscript prose. Distinguish new contributions from prior findings and supporting evidence.
